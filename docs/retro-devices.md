## Notes
- **Legal line (US / Puerto Rico, not legal advice):** emulators are legal; downloading copyrighted games/books you don't own is not.
  Modifying a console or e-reader's software is a grey area under the DMCA's anti-circumvention rule (the official
  exemptions cover phones and tablets, not game consoles or dedicated e-readers); it also voids warranties and breaks
  the makers' terms. Keep it to our own devices and our own content.
- **E-reader without modding:** a **Kobo** reads EPUB and PDF out of the box and accepts files over USB (and can run the
  open-source KOReader app); a regular **Kindle** can receive EPUB/PDF through Amazon's "Send to Kindle". Either works
  with the free/legal books in CaciqueLibrary (Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, OpenStax).
